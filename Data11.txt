# Practical 11: Customer Segmentation & Behavior Analysis (Amazon Dataset)

Source: Data_Visualization_Sahil.pdf

Objective: Identify customer segments based on purchasing patterns.

## (a) Use Clustering

Objective: To identify customer segments based on their purchasing patterns using clustering.

Steps

1. Open Amazon-Dataset.xlsx in Tableau.
2. Open a new worksheet.
3. Drag Sales to Columns.
4. Drag Profit to Rows.
5. Open the Analytics pane.
6. Under Model, drag Cluster onto the scatter plot.
7. In the Clusters dialog box, set the required number of clusters.
8. Click Apply.
9. Observe the different customer segments formed.

Result: Customer segments were created using clustering based on Sales and Profit.&#x20;

## (b) Analyze Frequency, Recency, and Monetary Value (RFM)

Objective: To analyze customer purchasing behavior using frequency, recency, and monetary value.

Steps

1. Open a new worksheet.
2. Drag Customer Name to Rows.
3. Drag Order Date to the view and use Count to analyze purchase frequency.
4. Use the latest Order Date to analyze customer recency.
5. Drag Sales to the view to analyze monetary value.
6. Compare customers based on frequency, recency, and sales.
7. Format the values and add suitable labels.

Result: Customer purchasing behavior was analyzed using Frequency, Recency, and Monetary Value (RFM).&#x20;

## (c) Create Scatter Plots and Segmentation Dashboards

Objective: To create scatter plots and an interactive dashboard for customer segmentation.

Steps

1. Open a new worksheet.
2. Drag Sales to Columns.
3. Drag Profit to Rows.
4. Drag Customer Name to Detail.
5. Open the Analytics pane and drag Cluster onto the view.
6. Observe the customer segments in the scatter plot.
7. Create a new dashboard.
8. Add the Scatter Plot and other customer analysis worksheets.
9. Add required filters and enable Use as Filter.
10. Add a suitable dashboard title.

Result: A scatter plot and interactive segmentation dashboard were created to analyze customer groups based on purchasing behavior.&#x20;

## (d) Highlight High-Value Customers

Objective: To identify and highlight high-value customers based on their sales.

Steps

1. Open a new worksheet.
2. Drag Customer Name to Rows.
3. Drag Sales to Columns.
4. Sort Sales from highest to lowest.
5. Drag Sales to Color.
6. Identify customers with the highest sales values.
7. Add suitable labels to highlight these customers.

Result: High-value customers were identified and highlighted based on their sales.&#x20;
