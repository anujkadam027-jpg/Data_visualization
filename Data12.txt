# Practical 12: Sales Forecasting and Trend Analysis (Amazon Dataset)

Source: Data_Visualization_Sahil.pdf

Objective: Predict future sales using historical data.

## (a) Build Time-Series Charts

Objective: To create time-series charts to analyze historical sales trends.

Steps

1. Open a new worksheet.
2. Drag Order Date to Columns.
3. Select Month or Year for the date level.
4. Drag Sales to Rows.
5. Tableau creates a line chart.
6. Add Region or Category to Color if required.
7. Observe the sales trend over time.

Result: A time-series chart was created to analyze historical sales trends.&#x20;

## (b) Apply Forecasting

Objective: To forecast future sales using historical sales data.

Steps

1. Open the time-series sales worksheet.
2. Open the Analytics pane.
3. Drag Forecast into the view.
4. Tableau adds the forecast to the sales trend.
5. Observe the predicted sales values for future periods.
6. Compare the forecast with the historical sales trend.

Result: A sales forecast was created using historical sales data to predict future sales.&#x20;

## (c) Compare Seasonal Trends

Objective: To compare seasonal sales trends using historical data.

Steps

1. Open a new worksheet.
2. Drag Order Date to Columns.
3. Select Month for the date level.
4. Drag Sales to Rows.
5. Drag Year to Color.
6. Compare the monthly sales patterns for different years.
7. Observe the months with higher or lower sales.

Result: Seasonal sales trends were compared across different years using monthly sales patterns.&#x20;

## (d) Use Moving Averages

Objective: To apply moving averages to smooth sales data and identify trends.

Steps

1. Open the time-series sales worksheet.
2. Drag Order Date to Columns.
3. Drag Sales to Rows.
4. Right-click Sales.
5. Select Quick Table Calculation → Moving Average.

Result: Moving averages were applied to smooth sales data and identify trends.&#x20;
