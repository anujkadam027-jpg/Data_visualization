## Quick memorization list

Focus on these formula names and patterns first:

- Total Revenue → Sales − Discount
- Profit Margin → Profit ÷ Sales × 100
- Average Sales → `AVG([Sales])`
- Total Profit → `SUM([Profit])`
- Sales by Region → `FIXED [Region]`
- Average Sales by Customer → `INCLUDE [Customer Name]`
- Sales Without Category → `EXCLUDE [Category]`
- High Profit → `IF … THEN … ELSE … END`
- Sales Without Null → `IFNULL([Sales], 0)`
- Profit Ratio → `SUM(Profit) / SUM(Sales)`
